02 · why this is not obvious

Two hypotheses, both plausible

Packing hurts

Divided attention. One reasoning budget, now five judgements.

Packing helps

Criteria in context. Verbose relative to helpful.

Pre-registered rival: position in the prompt, not how many share the call.

03 · the design decision that shapes everything

Why not judge versus judge

  • Packed vs unpacked measures two fallible instruments.
  • Both degrade together, it reports a clean null.
  • So: ground truth on both sides.
  • HelpSteer2 · 10,681 prompts × 2 responses · five criteria · 0.5 baseline

What is held constant

06 · the outcome nobody reports

The tie rate is co-primary,
and it is flat

The trap

A judge that stops committing looks identical to one that got better, if you drop the ties.

0.4800 → 0.4796

tie rate, K = 1 → K = 5

Report it next to accuracy. Always.

07 · why you should believe it

Estimators tested against a known answer

  • Clustered standard errors. Always.
  • Within-item paired contrasts. Primary.
  • Three estimators. They fail differently. All agree here.
  • Validated against planted effects.

Plant an effect. Check you find it. Plant nothing. Check you do not.

10 · what it took to measure this

Detection rate hides two different failures

Judged backwards, or saw no difference at all. Opposite remedies. One number cannot tell them apart.

detection

hits / n

tie rate

ties / n

discrimination

hits / (hits + wrong)

Insensitive, not inaccurate. Wrong-direction rates near zero, tie rates to 0.95.

16 · the limit

What this still cannot say

  • The estimand is 39 trailing items behind a first-position target. Load, recency and distance from the end all move together.
  • Eight operations chosen for applicability, not representativeness.
  • One judge, one corpus, and gpt-5-mini is an alias, not a snapshot.

Removed, not solved. The ordinal-position confound is gone. The rest of the bundle is not.

17 · the defensible claim

What I will actually claim

Five criteria in one call cost nothing measurable and save 46%. Forty behind a first-position target cost 7.7 points of recovery (95% CI −12.4 to −2.9). At twenty, the decline was position, not count.

And what I am not claiming

  • Not that load is the cause at forty. Recency moves with it.
  • Not which operations break.
  • Not generalisation past this judge, this corpus.

18 · takeaway

If you run judges in production

  • Pack to about five. Free, measured, metered.
  • Forty is too many. Now measured, not extrapolated.
  • Order your criteria before you unpack them.
  • Anchor every scale point.
  • Report the tie rate next to accuracy.
  • Plant an effect in your estimators before trusting them.
