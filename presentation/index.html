09 · the synthetic experiment

No new human labels

  • 1 measured target has a known direction.
  • 39 load-only metrics make the call larger.
  • Clean and corrupted responses are randomized.
  • The target stays first at every rung.

Eight operations contribute 58 prompts each. The outcome is recovery of the known edit.

12 · the scope

What this result measures

  • One targeted metric fixed at the front.
  • Up to 39 additional metrics appended behind it.
  • Eight known edits applied to HelpSteer2 responses.
  • gpt-5-mini using one fixed prompt and rubric.

A precise measurement of this setup, not a universal model limit.

13 · where does it break

It does not break. It bends.

gradual

no detectable cliff

I added until it broke. It was bending the whole time. 1% of log-odds per criterion, from the first one.

14 · the defensible claim

What I will actually claim

  • 5 metrics: no measurable loss, 46% cheaper.
  • 40 metrics: recovery lower by 6.3 points.
  • Between them: a gradual decline, not a cliff.

15 · takeaway

If you run judges in production

  • Pack about five metrics.
  • Retest quality as you add more.
  • Anchor every scale point.
  • Report the tie rate next to accuracy.
